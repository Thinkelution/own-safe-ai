A LITTLE CONTEXT GOES A LONG WAY
Save once.
Use where you need it.
OwnSafeAI Plugin is being built for the notes you choose to keep, and the moments you choose to use them.

      

        
01 / SAVE
“Add this to my Travel notes.”✓ A note worth keeping
Keep what matters.
A preference, a decision, a useful answer. Ask the plugin to save it in the right context.

        
02 / ORGANISE
↗ Travel▤ Work✳ Personal
Give each part of life a home.
Separate your travel plans from your work notes. Choose which context to bring into a conversation.

        
03 / USE
Your notes→Your next prompt + relevant context
Pick up where you left off.
Ask your AI tool to refer to your notes. Relevant context can travel with you, even when you switch tools.

      

    


    

      
YOUR NOTES, WITH YOU IN CONTROL
A memory you
can actually read.
We’re designing OwnSafeAI around editable notes. You should be able to see what’s saved, correct it, export it and remove it.
When you ask to use a context, the relevant notes will be shared with that AI tool. The product is designed around explicit saving and retrieval, with summaries linked back to the original notes.
